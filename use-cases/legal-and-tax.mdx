In legal and tax advisory, a single erroneous assumption carries catastrophic financial and legal liability. CONNEX’s citation-grounded architecture guarantees that all conclusions reference statutory precedents and agreement clauses.

<Steps>
  <Step title="1. Contract & Statutory Ingestion" icon="file-text">
    Ingests bilateral agreements, regulatory codes, and tax case precedent records into isolated VFS storage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2. Brain X Legal Grounding" icon="scale">
    Cross-references clauses against jurisdiction-specific statutory boundaries (PIPA, CCPA, GDPR).
  </Step>
  <Step title="3. Clause Auditing & Citation Fidelity" icon="shield-check">
    Generates verified risk matrices with chunk-level citations (`[ref:agreement#clause]`) for counsel review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

1. **Cross-Border Agreement Auditing**: Flags indemnification exposure, uncapped liability clauses, and unfavorable dispute resolution mechanisms.
