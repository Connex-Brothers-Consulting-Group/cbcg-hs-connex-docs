# Getting Started with CONNEX

CONNEX Cloud OS is accessible directly via modern web browsers without complex local installation requirements.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Jurisdictional Authentication">
    Navigate to the [CONNEX Web Application](https://connex.cloud). Select your statutory country of residence (Seoul KR region or Iowa US region) and sign in using sovereign OIDC or Google Identity credentials.

    <Info>
      Personal data is physically isolated in regional databases complying with Republic of Korea PIPA and US CCPA statutory requirements.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Workspace Domain">
    From the global left navigation drawer, choose your preferred workspace:
    - **CoWorx**: Real-time organizational team messaging and document sharing
    - **CONNEX Agent**: Autonomous AI reasoning, document generation, coding, and multimodal analytics
    - **VFS Storage**: Isolated virtual cloud file system explorer
  </Step>
  <Step title="Engage with Your Autonomous AI Partner">
    Enter your business inquiry into the prompt area, attach relevant VFS documents, and receive grounded, high-precision intelligence instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>
